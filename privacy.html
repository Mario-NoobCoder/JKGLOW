This policy explains how JK Glow collects, uses and protects your personal data when you browse this website or submit one of our contact forms.

Data we collect

  • Contact details you submit via forms: name, company, email, phone and city.
  • Business information related to partnership requests.
  • The content of any message you send us.

Purpose

We process your data to respond to your inquiries, manage B2B relationships and keep a record of our commercial exchanges. We do not use your data for unrelated purposes.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all. It uses no local storage, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The light or dark theme you select is applied by your browser for that single page view only, and nothing about that choice is saved or transmitted.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Contact form submissions are delivered to our mailbox through FormSubmit, which acts as the processor for that transmission only. Beyond that, we may share data with service providers strictly for communication and hosting purposes, and where required by law.

Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above or as required by applicable law.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. You may also withdraw your consent at any time.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@jkglow.com.
