About us

Building strong relationships directly with Korean beauty brands

JK Glow is a Madagascar-based distributor focused on selected Korean beauty brands. We support pharmacies, beauty professionals and retailers with reliable supply, brand education and commercial development.

  1. 01

    Who we are

    JK Glow is a Madagascar-based distributor focused on selected Korean beauty brands. We support pharmacies, beauty professionals and retailers with reliable supply, brand education and commercial development.

  2. 02

    Our journey

    From the creation of JK Glow to the launch of SKIN1004 in Madagascar, our journey has been guided by product quality and long-term partnerships. We are expanding our professional network and market coverage step by step.

  3. 03

    Our vision

    To develop access to high-quality, carefully selected K-Beauty in Madagascar and progressively across African markets.

How we work

Four commitments we do not compromise on

Authenticity

Genuine products, sourced through official brand relationships.

Reliability

Consistent supply for the professionals who count on us.

Education

We train retail and beauty teams so products are explained well.

Partnership

Long-term relationships over one-off transactions.

Want to work with us?

Whether you are a retailer or a Korean beauty brand, we would like to hear from you.
