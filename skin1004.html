How we work

Import, clear, and stock locally

We handle the parts that make retail hard in Madagascar: sea freight, customs, local warehousing and a small, reliable stock held in Antananarivo.

  1. 1

    Selection

    We review each brand, its demand in Madagascar and its margin structure before signing.

  2. 2

    Logistics

    Sea freight to Toamasina, customs clearance handled in-house, then transfer to our Antananarivo warehouse.

  3. 3

    Availability

    Core lines are kept in stock so a retailer can reorder without a six-week wait.

  4. 4

    Support

    Product training, merchandising guidance and reorder reminders for every partner.
